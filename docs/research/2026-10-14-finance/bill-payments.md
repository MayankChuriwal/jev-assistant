# India household bill payments from a desktop browser: desk research (14 Oct 2026)

Limits: WebSearch/WebFetch only; no logins, forms, terms or CAPTCHAs touched. The WebSearch budget ran out mid-run, so some items stay [unconfirmed]. Unreadable to my fetcher: amazon.in (503), au.bank.in (403), federal.bank.in (Radware bot challenge, not followed), JS-only pages (CRED, MobiKwik, Tata Power, BESCOM quick-pay, NHAI pass page). "Undated" = page shows no date (fetched 14 Oct 2026). NBBL circulars are cited by number/date; index: https://www.bharat-connect.com/circulars/.

## 1. Bharat Connect (BBPS), run by NBBL
- Rebranded Bharat Connect (NBBL circulars 11 Sep-20 Dec 2024). 22,755 live billers, 79 COUs, 55 BOUs [verified-primary] https://www.bharat-connect.com/statistics/ (1 Sep 2026).
- No NBBL pay site: "No separate registration is needed. Use existing bank credentials or any Bharat Connect partnering app" [verified-primary] https://www.bharat-connect.com (undated). My Bharat Connect is a bill-tracking dashboard, "not a payment application" https://www.bharat-connect.com/solutions/my-bharat-connect/ (undated).
- Categories (31 listed): Electricity, Water, DTH, Cable TV, Broadband, Landline, Mobile, Piped gas, LPG, Rent, Housing Society, Municipal, Credit Card, Loan Repayment, Insurance, NPS, Education, FASTag, NCMC, EV Recharge, eChallan, Forex, others [verified-primary] home page (undated).
- RBI-driven curbs, NPCI/2025-26/BBPS/009 (21 Aug 2025): Hospital & Pathology no fresh transactions from 31 Aug 2025; Donation no new billers; Housing Society/Rental no P2P; Recurring Deposits/Digital Gold existing billers out by 31 Dec 2025; Loan Repayment regulated entities only. AD1 (23 Mar 2026): unregulated lenders de-boarded, "No transactions… post 1st April 2026" [verified-primary].
- Modes: NEFT and IMPS disabled from 5 Jun 2025, Internet Banking stays (BBPS/003, 14 May 2025; AD1, 30 May 2025) [verified-primary]; NBBL's modes page still lists them (stale) https://www.bharat-connect.com/customers/payment-modes/. A credit card cannot fund Credit Card or Loan Repayment bills; UPI can (BBPS/023, 22 Dec 2023, older) [verified-primary].
- Flow: RBI Master Direction (29 Feb 2024, effective 1 Apr 2024) s4(d): "will require the bill to be fetched before payment initiation" [verified-primary] https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12616. NBBL Procedural Guidelines v3.0 (Mar 2024; 2026 circulars cite v6.0, not found): flows Fetch & Pay, Validation & Pay, Validation & Pay by plan, Direct Pay; amount per biller exact/above/below/any https://ebixcash.com/static/Common/pdf/bbps-procedural-guidelines-version-3.0-march-2024.pdf. Consumer steps: channel, biller, Customer ID/mobile + amount, mode, confirmation with "B assured" logo https://www.bharat-connect.com/customers/ (undated).
- Reference/receipt: every payment carries a BBPS reference from initiation (RBI MD s6(a)(4); BBPS/008, 16 Jul 2026: comply by 15 Sep 2026). Receipts via SMS/email/e-receipt with unique reference, checkable on website/SMS/IVRS; confirmation only acknowledges payment at the BBPS interface (Guidelines §5.3) [verified-primary].
- Complaints: https://www.bharat-connect.com/support/raise-complaint-customer/ (undated): form with query type (Refund; Failed transaction; Money debited but no confirmation; Transaction ID not received; Others), mobile, email, date, amount, 20-character Bharat Connect transaction reference ID, screenshot ≤5 MB. Login/OTP/CAPTCHA not mentioned; no resolution timeline published; escalation bbps.ops@npci.org.in, bbps@npci.org.in [verified-primary]. Backend "Canvas" CMS issues a Complaint Identifier [reported-secondary] PayU BBPOU policy v2.0, 10 Mar 2026 https://devguide.payu.in/website-assets/uploads/2026/03/Customer-Grievance-Redressal-Policy-of-PayU-Payments-Private-Limited-Bharat-Bill-Payment-System-BBPS.pdf. NBBL FAQ: payment cannot be changed or cancelled; refunds are requested from the biller or initiating entity (customers page). Refunds use BBPCU "Credit Adjustment" (Guidelines §6.2.1).
- Failed-transaction TAT, RBI/2019-20/67 (20 Sep 2019, effective 15 Oct 2019): auto-reversal outer limit T+1 (IMPS, UPI P2P, card-to-card) or T+5 (UPI merchant payment, PoS, ATM); ₹100/day beyond, credited without a complaint; no bill-payment row; no TAT for card-not-present [verified-primary] https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11693. RBI MD s8(iii) points BBPOUs to it. PayU cites an RBI "Harmonization of TAT for BBPS operations, 27 Mar 2025": not located [unconfirmed].
- "Deemed Success" (BBPS/007, 22 Aug 2024, by 30 Sep 2024): biller non-response was failure by default; now settled as success, "minimising instances of refunds" [verified-primary]. A success receipt does not prove biller posting.
- Escalation: RB-IOS 2026 (in force 1 Jul 2026): complain to the regulated entity first; wait 30 days or the RBI/NPCI timeline (higher); file within 90 days at cms.rbi.org.in (14448); covers banks, NBFCs, non-bank PPI issuers; PAs/BBPOUs not listed [verified-primary] https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/RBIOS01072026.pdf (1 Jul 2026).

## 3. Credit-card bills
- Mandate: media report an RBI direction (a Feb 2024 BBPS circular) that third-party apps route card-bill payments via BBPS from 1 Jul 2024; issuers' own site/net banking/app stayed allowed [reported-secondary] https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/no-credit-card-repayments-via-third-party-apps-this-is-what-it-means-for-you (1 Jul 2024). The RBI BBPS Master Direction has no credit-card clause; primary instrument not found [unconfirmed].
- NBBL CCBP rules (BBPS/005, 15 Aug 2024): fetch only "customer initiated", first fetch authenticated, reminders only on explicit time-bound consent; full card number, OTP, PIN never stored [verified-primary].
- Issuers: 18 live "as on 27 Aug 2024" (SBI Card, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, IndusInd, IDFC FIRST, Yes, Federal, AU, BoB, Canara and others) [reported-secondary, stale] https://bankbazaar.com/credit-card/how-to-pay-credit-card-bills-using-bbps.html. No 2026 list found [unconfirmed]; Credit Card is still a listed category.
- No-login: SBI Card PayNet "Continue Without Login": card number + amount, BillDesk, net banking/debit/UPI, "reflected instantly" https://www.sbicard.com/creditcards/app/user/paynet (undated). ICICI: UPI to ccpay.<10-digit mobile><last 4>@icici; portal needs login https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/blogs/card/credit-card/credit-card-payment-procedure (undated). HDFC: NetBanking/PayZapp need login; NetBanking "take 1 day"; PayZapp same day for HDFC cards, 2-4 days for others https://www.hdfc.bank.in/credit-cards/services/credit-cards-bill-payment (undated). ## 6. Automation facts
- Fetch codes (vendor docs, not NBBL): BFR004 "Payment received for the billing period, no bill due"; BVR008 "Customer account valid but no bill due"; BFR003 "No bill data available"; BFR002 invalid parameters; BFR007 due date expired (non-retriable); BFR008 biller unreachable, BOU003/006/007/008/009 timeouts, BPR010 downtime (retriable); BPR007 "Repeat payment request" (non-retriable); BPR011 "Payment amount different from current outstanding"; BPR008 re-fetch needed [reported-secondary] https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/other-apis/bbps-cou/error-codes (undated).
- Amount rules: per biller (section 1). Adani web offers "Minimum Payable"/"Partial Payment"; NEFT/RTGS exact only; TPDDL: no card part payment, net banking allows it; MSEDCL credits extra/advance to next bill; FASTag any amount above minimum [verified-primary, section 9 URLs].
- Duplicates: UPMS "eliminates multiple reminders and the risk of duplicate payment" (BBPS/013, 20 Nov 2024); MSEDCL "Do not open multiple payment windows simultaneously"; BSES "4 transactions per card per month"; TPDDL one card for up to five CA numbers per cycle; fetch-to-payment above 5:1 priced at agent level (BBPS/007/AD1, 29 Sep 2025, slabs deferred) [verified-primary].
- Reflection: Adani credits in "one working day", shows on its site after "3-5 working days"; BSES/TPDDL want card payments at least 3 days before due date; MSEDCL books the transaction completion date; inter-operator settlement 10 cycles/day from 1 Jun 2026 (BBPS/004, 15 May 2026) [verified-primary].
- Refund clocks: RBI table; Tata Play 7-10 working days; Paytm 21 working days if a recharge is undelivered after 24 hours; Freecharge 21 working days, report within 48 hours; Google Pay defers to billers [verified-primary, URLs above].
- LPG: BPCL booking must use the non-editable app-login mobile as "Registered Contact Number" (BBPS/005, 22 May 2026) [verified-primary].

## 7. 2025-2026 changes
- Data: remitter details above ₹50,000 (BBPS/002, 24 Jun 2025); accurate customer mobile on every transaction from 1 Nov 2025 (BBPS/010); on-us Bill Reference Id (BBPS/001, 1 Apr 2026) [verified-primary].
- Fees: CCF1, a biller charge "over and above the actual bill amount" (fixed, percentage or slab) on Steering-Committee-approved categories; front-ends must show it by 30 Jul 2026 (BBPS/007, 25 Jun 2026). Customer-side CCF is capped by BBPS (Guidelines §7.2); the Master Direction has no convenience-fee rule [verified-primary].
- New: Forex category (21 Aug 2025); UPMS for FASTag/NCMC auto-replenishment (18 Feb 2025); UPI AutoPay on fetch-enabled billers (BBPS/025, 21 Feb 2024); Banking Connect net-banking switch, Axis live 9 Apr 2026 [verified-primary] https://www.bharat-connect.com/strapi/uploads/NBBL_s_Banking_Connect_onboards_Axis_Bank_ca8b57bf4e.pdf.
- WhatsApp in-app bill payments (UPI, debit, credit card; 22,722 billers, 30 categories; gradual rollout) 3 Sep 2026 [reported-secondary] https://www.etvbharat.com/en/technology/whatsapp-introduces-a-new-bill-payment-feature-in-india-heres-how-to-make-payments-enn26090307184. "Any UPI app" is covered only by ClickPay/Bharat Connect QR; credit-line-on-UPI for bills: nothing found [unconfirmed].
- Agentic: NPCI/OpenAI/Razorpay pilot, reported Unified Agent Protocol (NPCI declined comment), bills not mentioned https://inc42.com/?p=570783 (1 Sep 2026); AiNxt/AtOM launched 9 Sep 2026, agent payments not covered https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/npci-launches-ainxt-and-atom-agentic-ai-platforms-for-upi-ecosystem (10 Sep 2026) [reported-secondary].
- Authentication: RBI/2025-26/79 (25 Sep 2025, effective 1 Apr 2026): two distinct factors, one dynamic for non-card-present; biometrics/device options allowed; SMS OTP not abolished [verified-primary] https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12898. OTP relay will not cover app-approval flows.
