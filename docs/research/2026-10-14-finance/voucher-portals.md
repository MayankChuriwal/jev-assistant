# India voucher portals for card-reward stacking: desk research (14 Oct 2026)

Limits: WebSearch/WebFetch/curl only. Nothing signed into, accepted or submitted. Pages that render only with JavaScript could not be read: ishoprewards.com terms, shopwise.giftstacc.com, grabdeals.axis.bank.in, and GyFTR's /tncs pages. Tags: [primary] = the operator's own page; [secondary] = blog or forum; [unconfirmed] = not verified. CAPTCHA is "none seen" only where our own runs or the page text show none.

## What changed in 2026
- **Amex Reward Multiplier ended on 30 Sep 2026.** ShopWise, the e-voucher store that replaced GyFTR on 1 Feb 2026, keeps "up to 5X" Membership Rewards points [secondary] https://1finance.co.in/blog/amex-reward-multiplier-discontinued-india/ (27 Aug 2026), https://livefromalounge.com/american-express-india-pulls-the-plug-on-reward-multiplier-effective-september-30-2026 (26 Aug 2026).
- **HDFC SmartBuy has a voucher sub-cap of 3,000 RP a month from 1 Jul 2026.** It covers vouchers from both GyFTR and Woohoo. Infinia's voucher cap was 15,000 before. Overall SmartBuy caps did not change [secondary] https://livefromalounge.com/hdfc-bank-credit-cards-devalues-smartbuy-gift-vouchers-accelerated-rewards-with-new-monthly-cap-effective-today/ (1 Jul 2026).
- **Axis gift-card purchases earn no rewards from 28 Aug 2026.** Axis now puts them in a separate "Gift card" category [secondary] https://www.insideflyer.com/posts/axis-bank-credit-card-rewards-and-fees-change-august-28/. It is not stated whether this hits Grab Deals or Gift EDGE purchases [unconfirmed].
- **GyFTR (Vouchagram India) runs white-label stores for many banks** on the same gyftr.com platform: HDFC /instantvouchers, Axis /edgerewards, SBI Card /sbicard, IndusInd /indusindinstavouchers, Yes /yesbank and /yesrewardz, StanChart /sc-instavouchers. AU's store is at rewardz.au.bank.in [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/. Our SmartBuy adapter is the template for all of them.
- **Kotak points moved to Kotak Unbox from 30 Jun 2026.** It is run by Reward360 (unbox.reward360.in) and opened from the bank app [primary] https://images.kotak.bank.in/bank/mailers/2026/files/Kotak_White_Migration_FAQ.pdf.

## Portals

**HDFC SmartBuy, GyFTR (supported).** URL: gyftr.com/instantvouchers. Sign-in: mobile OTP, asked at Add to cart. Payment: card on pay.gyftr.com. No CAPTCHA seen in our runs. Earns "Up to 5X reward points or 5% cashback" [primary] https://gyftr.com/hdfcsmartbuy/. The 3,000 RP voucher sub-cap above applies. SmartBuy also sells Woohoo vouchers (Marriott, for one) [secondary] https://savesage.club/blogs/hdfc-smartbuy-brand-voucher-cap-july-2026.

**ICICI iShop, Reward360 (supported).** URL: ishoprewards.com. Sign-in: ICICI net banking. Payment: credit card, debit card or net banking. Shopping vouchers earn 6X RP. Monthly caps are 15,000 RP (8,000 a day), or 18,000 (10,000 a day) on Emeralde Private Metal. Co-brand cards are capped at 1,100. Cards pay a 2.5% fee on Swiggy, Amazon and Flipkart vouchers. Per-brand limits: Amazon and Flipkart ₹12,000; BigBasket and Blinkit ₹2,000 [secondary] https://cardinsider.com/blog/icici-bank-ishop-rewards-portal/ (9 Oct 2026). Bot terms are JS-only [unconfirmed].

**SBI Card, GyFTR.** URL: gyftr.com/sbicard (also the SBI Card app's E-Store). Sign-in: SBI-registered mobile number. Payment: SBI credit or debit cards only. The site claims "up to 40%" off and "Earn upto 10% GV COINS"; coins last 6 months. Codes arrive by WhatsApp, SMS and email [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/sbicard. Discounts and coins need full payment by SBI or Tata card; corporate cards are excluded [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/sbicard/tnc. Base points use the card's normal rate. SimplyCLICK's 10X partner cap is 10,000 a month [primary] https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/simply-click-tnc-MITC-booklet.pdf.

**Axis Gift EDGE, GyFTR.** URL: gyftr.com/edgerewards. Sign-in: mobile number; card points can be used only through Axis net banking or the app. Payment: Axis credit or debit cards, EDGE points and GyFTR Coins. The site claims up to 50% off and up to 10% coins. Codes come by SMS or email in minutes [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/edgerewards. Real discounts are smaller: MakeMyTrip 5%, Cleartrip 6.5% [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/edgerewards/blog/?p=149 (2 Jan 2026). Base Axis points are probably lost from 28 Aug 2026.

**Axis Grab Deals.** URL: grabdeals.axis.bank.in. Its instant-voucher store is Woohoo's white-label at grabdealsaxisbank.woohoo.in, live with "© 2026 Pine Labs" [primary] https://grabdealsaxisbank.woohoo.in/ and [primary] https://www.axis.bank.in/docs/default-source/default-document-library/credit-card/grabdeals-tnc-howtouse.pdf (2024). Sign-in: registered mobile number plus the card's last 4 digits. Payment: the same Axis card in full. Cashback is capped at ₹1,000 a month and points at 5,000 EDGE RP a month. Example offers: Flipkart 5% or 5X; Zepto 2.5% [secondary] https://cardinsider.com/blog/axis-bank-grab-deals/ (1 Oct 2026).

**IndusInd and Yes, GyFTR.** IndusInd (gyftr.com/indusindinstavouchers): mobile OTP; IndusInd cards, UPI or net banking. It claims "up to 10X Reward Points" but most brands show 1–2X [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/indusindinstavouchers. Yes (gyftr.com/yesbank): mobile OTP; Yes cards. It claims up to 35% off [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/yesbank.

**Amex ShopWise.** URL: shopwise.giftstacc.com (a Giftstacc React app). Sign-in: Amex India online account, then Rewards & benefits, then Shop Now [secondary] https://1finance.co.in/blog/shopwise-new-evoucher-platform-for-amex-credit-card/ (9 Feb 2026). This route may change now that Reward Multiplier is gone [unconfirmed]. Payment: all cash or all MR points, never mixed. Up to 10 vouchers per transaction. Earns 5X, 3X or 2X by card, credited in about 48 hours. Codes come by email and SMS [secondary] https://cardinsider.com/blog/american-express-introduces-shopwise-new-reward-multiplier-partner/ (2 Feb 2026). Platinum Reserve earns 3X [secondary] https://book1a.com/blog/credit-cards/amex/platinum-reserve-review/. No cap published. Amex sign-in needs a password, which Jev never asks for. Amex SafeKey terms ban "any robot, spider" [primary] https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/in/benefits/SafeKey-TnCspdf/IndiaSafeKeyTCs.pdf. The main site's terms were not found.

**GyFTR main store.** URL: gyftr.com. Sign-in: phone and email. Payment: cards, UPI, net banking, wallets and GyFTR Pay; no international cards. Claims up to 40% off [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/. No bank multiplier, so it gives only the card's base rate, which many issuers exclude for gift cards. robots.txt allows everything [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/robots.txt. GyFTR terms grant use "solely for personal, noncommercial use". No bot clause found [primary] https://www.gyftr.com/godrejclubone/terms-and-conditions.

**Woohoo.in (Pine Labs).** Payment: "credit cards, debit cards and internet banking". Up to 10 units per order. Codes come by email and SMS [primary] https://www.woohoo.in/faq. Offers are by brand (Flipkart +2%, Ajio +8%). Sign-in method and CAPTCHA [unconfirmed]. Terms forbid you to "Aggregate or scrape any content… without our express written permission" and require "personal, non-commercial use only" [primary] https://woohoo.in/termsofuse.

**Kotak Unbox.** Reward360, opened from the Kotak app. Solitaire earned 10 miles per ₹100 on vouchers, accelerated part capped at 10,000 a month, 1 Jul–30 Sep 2026 (ended) [secondary] https://cardinsider.com/blog/kotak-solitaire-credit-card-offers-air-miles-on-voucher-purchases-via-kotak-unbox/. A desktop web entry [unconfirmed].

**Zingoy.** Sign-in: Google, Apple, Facebook or email with password. Offers cashback by brand; payment MCC 5947 or 5310 [secondary] https://cardexpress.in/mcc-guide/lookup/zingoy. Terms ban "robot, spider, or another automatic device" [primary] https://www.zingoy.com/terms. **Excluded.**

**Amazon Pay and Flipkart gift cards** (issuers, not portals for stacking rewards).
- Amazon: ₹10–10,000 per card and 5 per order. Credit-card purchases are capped at ₹50,000 per 30 days, and Amazon may turn off card purchases for an account [secondary] https://zoutons.com/news/amazon-pay-gift-card-rules-2026 (12 Oct 2026). Its terms exclude "data mining, robots, or similar data gathering and extraction tools" [primary, US copy] https://conductatlas.com/platform/amazon/amazon-conditions-of-use/provision/CA-P-018441/prohibition-on-data-mining-and-scraping-tools/. Amazon sued Perplexity over the Comet browser agent. An injunction was granted in Mar 2026 and vacated by the 9th Circuit in Aug 2026 [secondary] https://www.retail-insight-network.com/newsletters/us-court-overturns-amazon-injunction-perplexity.
- Flipkart: card, debit or net banking only. Limits: ₹10,000 per card, 100 cards and ₹1 lakh per month [primary] https://www.flipkart.com/flipkart-digital-gift-card/p/itmf766ce1fa88cd. Terms reportedly bar "deep-link, page-scrape, robot, spider" [secondary] https://www.itechguides.com/?p=863860. **High risk.**

**Dropped**
- Magicpin, PhonePe, Paytm and SaveSage gift cards are app-first and offers vary by user [secondary] https://www.desidime.com/deals/magicpin-get-flat-7-2083612.
- IDFC FIRST has only an in-app catalogue for points [primary] https://www.idfcfirst.bank.in/rewards-gallery.
- "MaxGyftr" was not found; it is likely GyFTR.
- Amex Reward Multiplier has ended.
