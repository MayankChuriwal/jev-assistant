- NPCI's MyUPI portal lists every UPI AutoPay mandate (subscriptions paid by UPI: Netflix, Spotify, ChatGPT, …) across all the user's UPI apps, and starts cancelling, pausing or resuming one. The user may ask what mandates they have, about one of them (amount, app, how often, how much paid so far), or to cancel, pause or resume one.
- The tab stays signed in between requests: when the page already shows MyUPI's chat or its UPI Autopay list, don't sign in again.
- Sign-in, in this order: type the user's phone ([profile:phone]) into the mobile number box ("Eg. 9876543210"); the captcha beside "Security Verification" goes to the user as a picture, and the text they read goes into that box; tick the "I have read and agree" checkbox; press Continue. The 6-digit code sent by SMS then goes into the one-time code boxes through the hidden field; press Proceed.
- A wrong captcha or an expired code shows an error: press Refresh CAPTCHA (or Resend code) and ask again; never guess.
- After sign-in MyUPI is a chat; UPI Autopay (or Show Autopay) lists the mandates as cards, a few at a time, each with its payee, amount, UPI app, linked bank, frequency, times executed, total paid and valid-till date. Scroll the list once from top to bottom and note each card's payee, amount, app and frequency; never scroll back and forth.
- To answer what mandates the user has or what they pay: DONE listing every card you noted (payee, amount, UPI app, frequency), not only the ones on screen. To answer about one mandate: DONE with that card's details.
- A card is the merchant's only when its payee names the merchant, or the company known to bill for that brand (ChatGPT bills as OPENAI LLC; YouTube Premium and Google One as GOOGLE). An app store's mandate (GOOGLE PLAY, APPLE) pays for any of the user's app subscriptions: it is never a brand's unless the user names the store. Never guess which mandate pays for what.
- Each card's buttons name its card, as Cancel (in “PAYEE”), Pause (in “PAYEE”): press only the one on the card you picked, and only the action the user asked for. A pause is not a cancellation.
- Cancel (or Pause, or Resume) opens the steps for it. For a cancellation the page says "To cancel, you'll need to authenticate via your UPI app" and shows a QR: nothing changes until the user approves it in their UPI app with their UPI PIN. A pause may first ask until when: use the date the user gave, else ASK them.
- At that QR (or when the page says to approve in the UPI app): TELL the user once to scan it with their UPI app and approve it there with their UPI PIN, naming the payee, amount and frequency, and that nothing changes until they do; the system sends them the QR's picture. Then WAIT for the page to show it done; don't tell them again.
- If the page doesn't change after a few waits, ASK whether they approved it in their UPI app. When they say not now, DONE saying nothing was changed and the mandate stays as it was.
- When no card's payee is the merchant or its company: DONE saying no UPI AutoPay mandate for it was found, listing every payee with its amount and UPI app; it may be billed by card, or inside an app store's mandate (say which store mandates there are, and that cancelling one would stop every app it pays for). Never press any card's buttons then.
- Report what was done (payee, amount, frequency, UPI app, any reference shown); for a cancellation, that future UPI payments to it stop and the merchant's own account isn't closed by this.
