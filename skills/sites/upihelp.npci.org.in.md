- NPCI's MyUPI portal lists every UPI AutoPay mandate (subscriptions paid by UPI: Netflix, Spotify, YouTube Premium, …) across all the user's UPI apps, and starts their cancellation.
- Sign-in, in this order: type the user's phone ([profile:phone]) into the mobile number box ("Eg. 9876543210"); the captcha beside "Security Verification" goes to the user as a picture, and the text they read goes into that box; tick the "I have read and agree" checkbox; press Continue. The 6-digit code sent by SMS then goes into the one-time code boxes through the hidden field; press Proceed.
- A wrong captcha or an expired code shows an error: press Refresh CAPTCHA (or Resend code) and ask again; never guess.
- After sign-in MyUPI is a chat: it lists the user's mandates, each with its payee, amount and frequency, or shows them under Mandate Management. Find the one whose payee is the merchant the goal names.
- Its cancel (or revoke) control starts the cancellation. The page then says "To cancel, you'll need to authenticate via your UPI app" and shows a QR: nothing is cancelled until the user approves it in their UPI app with their UPI PIN.
- At that QR: TELL the user to scan it with their UPI app and approve the cancellation of that merchant's AutoPay there with their UPI PIN, naming the amount and frequency, and that nothing is cancelled until they do; the system sends them the QR's picture. Then WAIT for the page to show the mandate cancelled or revoked.
- If the page doesn't change after a few waits, ASK whether they approved it in their UPI app. When they say not now, DONE saying nothing was cancelled and the mandate stays active.
- A pause is not a cancellation: never pause.
- When no mandate for that merchant is listed: DONE saying no UPI AutoPay mandate for it was found, quoting the payees that are listed.
- Report what was cancelled (merchant, amount, frequency, UPI app, any reference shown) and that future UPI payments to it stop; the merchant's own account isn't closed by this.
