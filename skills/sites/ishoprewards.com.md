- ICICI Bank's rewards portal, run by Reward360. It opens with its terms in a pop-up ("Accept & Proceed"): the system presses it.
- Shopping vouchers: https://www.ishoprewards.com/shopping-vouchers lists brands with the points or iCash each earns. The search box at the top ("Search …") finds a brand: type its name ("Swiggy") and press its suggestion, which opens the brand's page ("Swiggy Money E-Voucher").
- A brand's page shows its amounts ("₹1000", "₹2000"), "Quantity - 1 +", the Total, "Add to cart" and "Buy now", and what it earns (points or iCash).
- A goal to show a brand's vouchers ends with DONE listing every amount and what it earns. It presses nothing on the brand's page: never an amount, Add to cart or Buy now.
- To buy: press the amount the goal names, set the quantity with − and +, then press "Buy now". Signed out, a pop-up says "Please login using ICICI Bank Net Banking": press "Proceed to Login". ICICI's page signs in and brings the user back to iShop.
- Checkout ("Proceed to Checkout" from the cart) shows the voucher, "Send order updates to", "Choose Payment Mode" (Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking), and Payment Details (Total Amount, Balance Payable). The payment mode is the user's to pick: ASK which, with those three as options, unless they said. Leave iCash and reward points unused unless they asked. "Proceed to Pay" (or "Pay Now", "Continue to Payment") is the final control: the system asks the user before it. Never enter card details.
- Report the voucher, its amount and quantity, the amount payable and what it earns, as the page shows them.
