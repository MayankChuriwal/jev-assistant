- ICICI Bank's rewards portal, run by Reward360. It opens with its terms in a pop-up ("Accept & Proceed"): the system presses it.
- Shopping vouchers: https://www.ishoprewards.com/shopping-vouchers lists brands with the points or iCash each earns. The search box at the top ("Search …") finds a brand: type its name ("Swiggy") and press its suggestion, which opens the brand's page ("Swiggy Money E-Voucher").
- A brand's page shows its amounts ("₹1000", "₹2000"), "Quantity - 1 +", the Total, "Add to cart" and "Buy now", and what it earns (shopping vouchers: 6X reward points on ICICI cards, capped at 15,000 a month). Its iCash (iShop's own wallet credit) is another offer than the points: put the points in reward; only when the brand earns iCash alone, put the iCash in reward. Never put iCash in discount. Cards pay a 2.5% fee on Swiggy, Amazon and Flipkart vouchers: put it in fee when the page shows it.
- To buy: press each amount the goal names, set its quantity with − and +, then "Buy now". Signed out, a pop-up says "Please login using ICICI Bank Net Banking": press "Proceed to Login". ICICI's page asks for the User ID ([profile:icici_user_id]) and the password ([ask]), then brings the user back to iShop.
- Checkout ("Proceed to Checkout" from the cart) shows the voucher, "Send order updates to", "Choose Payment Mode" (Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking) and Payment Details (Total Amount, Balance Payable). Choose Credit Card (the goal names the card); type [ask] into the card boxes. Leave iCash and reward points unused. "Proceed to Pay" (or "Pay Now", "Continue to Payment") pays: the system asks the user before it.
